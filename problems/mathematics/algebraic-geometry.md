## Q208. For every simple matroid \$M\$ on \$[n]\$, are the Gysin maps \$\bigoplus_D H^{k-2}(D…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** conjecture (Conjecture 8.1.5) · **Collection** 3

For every simple matroid \$M\$ on \$[n]\$, are the Gysin maps \$\bigoplus_D H^{k-2}(D,\mathbb Q)\to H^k(X_{\Sigma_M},\mathbb Q)\$ jointly surjective for every \$k>0\$? Here \$\Sigma_M\$ has cones generated by \$e_F=\sum_{i\in F}e_i\$ along chains of nonempty proper flats in \$\mathbb R^n/\mathbb R(1,\ldots,1)\$; \$X_{\Sigma_M}\$ is its complex toric variety, and \$D\$ ranges over irreducible torus-invariant divisors. Simple means no loops or parallel elements.

**Context.** Origin: New divisor-generation conjecture; its equivalent monomial-cycle formulation is counted once.

**Source.** Kyle Binder. *Tor Groups of the Stanley–Reisner Ring of a Matroid*. The Ohio State University, 2025. Advisor(s): Eric Katz. [primary source](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/ws/send_file/send?accession=osu1744360382902721&disposition=inline) · [record](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/r/etd/search/10?clear=10&p10_accession_num=osu1744360382902721) Location: Conjecture 8.1.5, printed p. 125 / PDF p. 136; equivalent to Conjecture 8.1.4 by Proposition 8.1.6 (counted once).

## Q390. For integers \$g\ge2\$ and \$k\ge1\$ with \$\rho(g,1,k)=2k-g-2\ge0\$, determine the di…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 4

For integers \$g\ge2\$ and \$k\ge1\$ with \$\rho(g,1,k)=2k-g-2\ge0\$, determine the dimension of the locus \$M_{g,k}\subset\mathcal M_g\$ of smooth complex genus-\$g\$ curves \$C\$ for which every component of \$G^1_k(C)\$ is nonreduced. Here \$G^1_k(C)\$ is the scheme of degree-\$k\$ pencils (two-dimensional spaces of sections of degree-\$k\$ line bundles). The thesis particularly highlights \$\rho\in\\{0,1\\}\$.

**Context.** Discovery path (advisor ascent; student → supervisor):

## Q1111. K-stability of every smooth Fano hypersurface

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Problem 6) · **Collection** 12

For all 3≤d≤n+1, is every smooth degree-d hypersurface X⊂P^{n+1} K-stable?

**Context.** Origin for questions 1111, 1112, 1113: Source-stated K-stability problems; the volume-gap problem goes back to Spotti–Sun. Setup for questions 1111, 1112, 1113: Work over C. For an n-dimensional klt germ x∈X, its normalized volume is inf_v A_X(v)^n vol(v), over real valuations centered at x, where vol(v)=lim_{m→∞} n! length(O_{X,x}/{f:v(f)≥m})/m^n.

**Source.** Chenyang Xu; Ziquan Zhuang. *Open problems in K-stability of Fano varieties*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.15576v2) Location: Problem 6.

**Literature check.** Status for questions 1111, 1112, 1113, checked 6 October 2026: June 2026 source retains all three. Later searches found restricted hypersurface and general special Gushel–Mukai results, not these full statements.

**Further links.** [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15576) · [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03311) · [3](https://epiga.episciences.org/18703)

## Q1212. Uniform positive F-signature for characteristic-p reductions

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** conjecture (Conjecture 8.2) · **Collection** 13

After localizing A, must there exist C>0 such that s(X_s,L_s)≥C for every closed point s of residue characteristic p and all sufficiently large p?

**Context.** Origin for question 1212: Carvajal-Rojas–Schwede–Tucker’s positivity question, in the authors’ projective formulation. Setup for question 1212: Fix a normal complex projective variety X of globally F-regular type and an ample line bundle L. Choose a model (X_A,L_A) over a finitely generated Z-subalgebra A⊂C. For a closed point s, s(X_s,L_s) is the F-signature of the corresponding section ring. Globally F-regular type means globally F-regular reductions on a dense subset.

**Source.** Seungsu Lee; Suchitra Pande. *The F-signature Function on the Big Cone*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.36321v1) Location: Conjecture 8.2 and §8 setup.

**Literature check.** Status for question 1212, checked 6 October 2026: Explicitly retained as Conjecture 8.2 on September 28,2026. Theorem 8.3 transfers an assumed bound between divisors; it does not prove existence generally.

**Further links.** [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36321) · [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16636)

## Q3456. Does tropical duality localize to stars?

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Question I.5.14) · **Collection** 35

If (Σ,w) satisfies tropical Poincaré duality over R, must every d-dimensional extended star γ⪰, with induced weights, also satisfy it?

**Context.** Origin: Doctoral restatement of the author’s 2023 article, Question 5.14. Setup: For a commutative ring R, let (Σ,w) be a finite pure d-dimensional rational polyhedral fan in a lattice N, with non-zero-divisor weights and closed weighted fundamental chain. Its multitangent cosheaf is F_p(σ)=(Σ\_{τ⊇σ}∧^p(N∩span_ℝ τ))⊗\_Z R; F^p is its R-dual. Tropical Poincaré duality means that cap product with [Σ,w] gives isomorphisms H^q(Σ,F^p)→H^{BM}\_{d−q}(Σ,F_{d−p}) for all p,q. For γ∈Σ, its extended star γ⪰ consists of cones σ+span_ℝ(γ) for σ⊇γ, subdivided with a common vertex.

**Source.** Edvard Aksnes. *Tropical homology manifolds*. University of Oslo, 2024. Advisor(s): Kris Shaw, Kristian Ranestad. [primary source](https://edvardak.github.io/slides/thesis/thesis.pdf) Location: Thesis Question I.5.14, p. 56; definitions I.3.6, I.3.11–12 and I.4.1.

**Literature check.** Status checked 9 October 2026: Thesis and published companion leave the question open; checked 2026-10-09 without locating a later resolution.

## Q3462. Minimal-weight compactifications of marked del Pezzo surfaces

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** conjecture (Conjecture 8.3.5) · **Collection** 35

For n=7,8, does K_c give the log-canonical compactification of Y_n, where respectively c=1/28+ε and c=1/240+ε for sufficiently small rational ε>0?

**Context.** Origin: Hacking–Keel–Tevelev 2009, Remark 1.3(4), restated in the thesis. Setup: Over C, let Y_n parametrize smooth marked del Pezzo surfaces of degree 9−n; a marking identifies the surface as the blowup of n ordered plane points. Let B be the labeled sum of its (−1)-curves. Write K_c for the normalization of the closure of the smooth marked pairs (S,cB), with B normal crossings, in the marked KSBA moduli space of stable pairs (semi-log-canonical, with K_S+cB ample).

**Source.** Nolan Schock. *Geometry of tropical compactifications of moduli spaces*. University of Georgia, 2022. Advisor(s): Valery Alexeev. [primary source](https://openscholar.uga.edu/record/4080/files/schock_nolan_202205_phd.pdf) Location: Conjecture 8.3.5 and Table 8.3, p. 172.

**Literature check.** Status checked 9 October 2026: Cases n=5, 6 are known. Latest Schock weighted-pair work treats those degrees; no solution for n=7, 8 located through 2026-10-09.

## Q3528. A toric Zariski–Nagata equality

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Question 1) · **Collection** 36

For every such X_Σ, closed V⊂X_Σ, and n≥1, must I(V)^{(n)}=⋂\_{p∈V}I(p)^n?

**Context.** Advisors: Jack Jeffries. Source type: PhD dissertation Origin: Smooth complete surface case of the author’s Question 1, explicitly revisited in the conclusion. Setup: Let X_Σ be a smooth complete complex toric surface, S=ℂ[x_ρ:ρ∈Σ(1)] its Cox ring, and π:ℂ^{Σ(1)}∖V(B_Σ)→X_Σ its Cox quotient. For a closed subvariety V, I(V) is the ideal generated by homogeneous polynomials vanishing on π^{-1}(V); write I(p)=I({p}). For radical I⊂S set I^{(n)}=⋂\_{P∈Min(I)}(I^nS_P∩S).

**Source.** Jordan Vincent Barrett. *A Zariski-Nagata Theorem for Smooth Toric Surfaces*. University of Nebraska–Lincoln, 2025. Advisor(s): Jack Jeffries. [primary source](https://digitalcommons.unl.edu/dissunl/294/) Location: Question 1, p.35; conclusion p.59; Definitions 4.2.19–20, pp.33–34.

**Literature check.** Status: The May 2025 dissertation proves sufficient cases and leaves the general smooth-surface case open. No later resolution found, checked 2026-10-09.
