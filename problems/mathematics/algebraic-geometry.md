## Q208. For every simple matroid \$M\$ on \$[n]\$, are the Gysin maps \$\bigoplus_D H^{k-2}(D…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** conjecture (Conjecture 8.1.5) · **Collection** 3

For every simple matroid \$M\$ on \$[n]\$, are the Gysin maps \$\bigoplus_D H^{k-2}(D,\mathbb Q)\to H^k(X_{\Sigma_M},\mathbb Q)\$ jointly surjective for every \$k>0\$? Here \$\Sigma_M\$ has cones generated by \$e_F=\sum_{i\in F}e_i\$ along chains of nonempty proper flats in \$\mathbb R^n/\mathbb R(1,\ldots,1)\$; \$X_{\Sigma_M}\$ is its complex toric variety, and \$D\$ ranges over irreducible torus-invariant divisors. Simple means no loops or parallel elements.

**Context.** Origin: New divisor-generation conjecture; its equivalent monomial-cycle formulation is counted once.

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Problem 6) · **Collection** 12

**Context.** Origin for questions 1111, 1112, 1113: Source-stated K-stability problems; the volume-gap problem goes back to Spotti–Sun. Setup for questions 1111, 1112, 1113: Work over C. **Context.** Origin for question 1212: Carvajal-Rojas–Schwede–Tucker’s positivity question, in the authors’ projective formulation.
