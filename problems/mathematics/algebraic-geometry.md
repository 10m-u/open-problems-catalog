## Q208. For every simple matroid \$M\$ on \$[n]\$, are the Gysin maps \$\bigoplus_D H^{k-2}(D…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** conjecture (Conjecture 8.1.5) · **Collection** 3

For every simple matroid \$M\$ on \$[n]\$, are the Gysin maps \$\bigoplus_D H^{k-2}(D,\mathbb Q)\to H^k(X_{\Sigma_M},\mathbb Q)\$ jointly surjective for every \$k>0\$? Here \$\Sigma_M\$ has cones generated by \$e_F=\sum_{i\in F}e_i\$ along chains of nonempty proper flats in \$\mathbb R^n/\mathbb R(1,\ldots,1)\$; \$X_{\Sigma_M}\$ is its complex toric variety, and \$D\$ ranges over irreducible torus-invariant divisors. **Context.** Origin for questions 1111, 1112, 1113: Source-stated K-stability problems; the volume-gap problem goes back to Spotti–Sun. Setup for questions 1111, 1112, 1113: Work over C. ## Q3456. Does tropical duality localize to stars?

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Question I.5.14) · **Collection** 35

If (Σ,w) satisfies tropical Poincaré duality over R, must every d-dimensional extended star γ⪰, with induced weights, also satisfy it?

**Context.** Origin: Doctoral restatement of the author’s 2023 article, Question 5.14. Setup: For a commutative ring R, let (Σ,w) be a finite pure d-dimensional rational polyhedral fan in a lattice N, with non-zero-divisor weights and closed weighted fundamental chain. Its multitangent cosheaf is F_p(σ)=(Σ\_{τ⊇σ}∧^p(N∩span_ℝ τ))⊗\_Z R; F^p is its R-dual. Tropical Poincaré duality means that cap product with [Σ,w] gives isomorphisms H^q(Σ,F^p)→H^{BM}\_{d−q}(Σ,F_{d−p}) for all p,q. For γ∈Σ, its extended star γ⪰ consists of cones σ+span_ℝ(γ) for σ⊇γ, subdivided with a common vertex.

**Source.** Edvard Aksnes. *Tropical homology manifolds*. University of Oslo, 2024. Advisor(s): Kris Shaw, Kristian Ranestad. [primary source](https://edvardak.github.io/slides/thesis/thesis.pdf) Location: Thesis Question I.5.14, p. 56; definitions I.3.6, I.3.11–12 and I.4.1.

**Literature check.** Status checked 9 October 2026: Thesis and published companion leave the question open; checked 2026-10-09 without locating a later resolution.

## Q3931. Can a symmetric numerical semigroup fail to be representable?

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Question 5.2.1) · **Collection** 40

Can a symmetric numerical semigroup fail to be representable?

**Context.** A numerical semigroup S is an additive submonoid of the nonnegative integers with finite complement. For S≠N, let F be its largest gap; S is symmetric if, for every integer n, n∈S exactly when F−n∉S. Call S representable if S={ℓ≥0:A_ℓ≠0} for the coordinate ring A, graded by a good effective C\* action, of a complex normal weighted-homogeneous surface singularity whose link is a rational homology sphere.

**Source.** Zsolt Baja and Tamás László. *Flat semigroups and weighted homogeneous surface singularities*. 2026. [primary source](https://doi.org/10.2140/agt.2026.26.201) Location: Question 5.2.1, p.224; setup §§1.1, 2.2–2.3.

**Literature check.** Status: Paper-origin question. ## Q4063. Are abelian surfaces X,Y derived isogenous exactly when there exists a quasiliftable principal…

**Context.** Work over algebraically closed k of characteristic p>3. Within abelian surfaces, or within K3 surfaces, “derived isogenous” means connected by a finite chain of k-linear equivalences between bounded derived categories of Brauer-twisted coherent sheaves; consecutive equivalences may use different Brauer classes on their shared surface.
