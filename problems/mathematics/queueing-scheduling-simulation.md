## Q2997. Monotonicity of counter-update speed

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Problem 1) · **Collection** 30

Does V(N+1)≤V(N) hold for every integer N≥2?

**Context.** Origin for questions 2997, 2998: Authors’ open problems. Setup for questions 2997, 2998: For N≥2 integer-valued counters, independently choose a uniform unordered pair at each discrete step. Increase the smaller counter by one; if equal, increase both. Let V(N) be the stationary expected total increment per step, using the positive-recurrent relative-gap process.

**Source.** Denis Denisov; Seva Shneer; Vitali Wachtel. *A model of discrete interacting updates*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.05913v2) Location: §2.4, Problem 1, p. 8.
