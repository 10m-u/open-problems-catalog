## Q207. Fix an odd prime \$p\$ and, separately, splitting type inert or split; also allow …

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 3

Fix an odd prime \$p\$ and, separately, splitting type inert or split; also allow ramified when \$p\ge5\$. Among real quadratic fields \$K\$ of that type ordered by discriminant \$d_K\$, does the proportion with \$v_p(\log_p\varepsilon_K)=k+c\$ tend to \$(1-1/p)p^{-k}\$ for every \$k\ge0\$? Here \$\varepsilon_K\$ is a fundamental unit in a \$p\$-adic embedding, \$v_p(p)=1\$, and \$c=1\$ for inert/split or \$c=1/2\$ for ramified.

**Context.** Origin: New quadratic-field distribution conjectures, extending earlier odd-prime-degree work. Submitted in 2025; defended in January 2026.

## Q290. Let X start at zero in R^d and be a Lévy process with nonsingular Brownian covar…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** conjecture (Conjecture II.15) · **Collection** 3

Let X start at zero in R^d and be a Lévy process with nonsingular Brownian covariance Sigma, observed until time 1. Let tau be the last time at which X or its left limit has maximal Euclidean norm, and let M be that maximizing position. Do the two paths sqrt(n)[X((tau − t/n)−) − M] and sqrt(n)[X(tau + t/n) − M], killed when they leave the observation interval, converge jointly in the Skorokhod topology to two conditionally independent Sigma-covariance Brownian motions conditioned to remain in the inward half-space {x: x·(−M) > 0}? The direction is mixed according to an independent copy of the law of −M.

## Q295. For every prime power q, is algebraic realizability of CI patterns over F_q char…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Question 4.49) · **Collection** 3

For every prime power q, is algebraic realizability of CI patterns over F_q characterized by excluding finitely many pattern minors, up to relabelling? The cases q = 2 and q = 3 are established; the question concerns the remaining finite fields.

**Context.** Origin: Original Gaussian-CI questions. The two-global-statements conjecture also appears in the author’s 2022 paper, beyond its proved two-local-statements theorem. Shared setup for questions 295, 296, 297, 298: A CI pattern on [n] comprises symbols (ij|K) whose almost-principal minors det Σ[iK,jK] vanish, for distinct i,j∉K. ## Q298. Is every inclusion-minimal gaussoid containing the local symbols prescribed by a…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** conjecture (Conjecture 4.66) · **Collection** 3

Is every inclusion-minimal gaussoid containing the local symbols prescribed by at most two global CI statements the exact CI pattern of some positive-definite real covariance matrix?

**Context.** Origin: Original Gaussian-CI questions. The two-global-statements conjecture also appears in the author’s 2022 paper, beyond its proved two-local-statements theorem. Shared setup for questions 295, 296, 297, 298: A CI pattern on [n] comprises symbols (ij|K) whose almost-principal minors det Σ[iK,jK] vanish, for distinct i,j∉K. Algebraic realization over F requires symmetric Σ with every principal minor nonzero. Positive realization over ordered F requires all principal minors positive; over R this means positive definite. Variables are labelled. Pattern minors delete variables and/or condition on a disjoint set C, translating (ij|K) into (ij|K∪C). Setup for question 298: A gaussoid is a set of local CI symbols closed under the following rules, for distinct i,j,k outside L: (ij|L) and (ik|jL) imply (ik|L) and (ij|kL); (ij|kL) and (ik|jL) imply (ij|L) and (ik|L); (ij|L) and (ik|L) imply (ij|kL) and (ik|jL); (ij|L) and (ij|kL) imply at least one of (ik|L),(jk|L). Here jL means L union {j}. A global statement (I,J|K), for pairwise disjoint I,J,K, means all symbols (ij|K) with i in I and j in J.

**Source.** Tobias Boege. *The Gaussian Conditional Independence Inference Problem*. Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg, 2022. Advisor(s): Thomas Kahle; Volker Kaibel.
