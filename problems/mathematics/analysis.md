**Context.** Doctoral context for question 1135: Dongwei Chen. PhD, Clemson University, 2024. Advisor: Martin Schmoll. **Source.** Olga Katkova; Anna Vishnyakova. *Convolution operators preserving the set of totally positive sequences*. 2025. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06468) Location: Conjecture 1.12

Square-integrable means some nonzero matrix coefficient belongs to L²(Ξ).
