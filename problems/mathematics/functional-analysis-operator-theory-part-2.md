## Q4124. Does X have the strong ball-covering property: are there xₙ∈X and radii rₙ>0 with supₙrₙ<∞,…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Question 5.5) · **Collection** 42

Does X have the strong ball-covering property: are there xₙ∈X and radii rₙ>0 with supₙrₙ<∞, ||xₙ||≥rₙ, and {x:||x||=1}⊆⋃ₙ{x:||x−xₙ||&lt;rₙ}?

**Context.** Let T={z∈C:|z|=1} have the chordal metric d(z,w)=|z−w| and basepoint 1. Let X=Lip₀(T) be the real Banach space of real Lipschitz functions f on T with f(1)=0, normed by ||f||Lip=sup_z≠w|f(z)−f(w)|/|z−w|.

**Source.** Johann Langemets, Emma Mõttus and Natalia Saealle. *Dual Banach spaces with the ball-covering property*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.10409v1) Location: Question 5.5, p. 25.

**Literature check.** Status: Paper-origin question. Ordinary ball-covering holds, while uniform ball-covering fails. Uniform separation of these balls from zero is not required here. Chordal distance cannot be replaced with arc-length distance in this question. No matching resolution found, 10 October 2026.

## Q4125. Characterize, in terms of d and f, exactly which such self-maps f have hypercyclic T_f.

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Problem 5.1) · **Collection** 42

Characterize, in terms of d and f, exactly which such self-maps f have hypercyclic T_f.

**Context.** Let (M,d,0) be a countable pointed metric space whose every point except possibly 0 is isolated, and let f:M→M be Lipschitz with f(0)=0. Define real Lip₀(M) using the Lipschitz-constant norm and F(M)=closure span{δₓ:x∈M}⊆Lip₀(M)\*, where δₓ(g)=g(x). The bounded linear operator T_f:F(M)→F(M) is determined by T_fδₓ=δ\_f(x). It is hypercyclic if some μ∈F(M) has norm-dense orbit {T_fⁿμ:n≥0}.

**Source.** Christian Cobollo, Romuald Ernst, Quentin Menet and Alfred Peris. *Hypercyclicity and Lipschitz-free operators*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17874v1) Location: Problem 5.1, p. 27.

**Literature check.** Status: Paper-origin classification question. The targeting criterion is proved only under additional metric or basis assumptions, and the paper explicitly leaves the full countable isolated-nonzero setting unresolved. No completeness, uniform discreteness or Schauder-basis assumption is added to the stated problem. This is separate from whether hypercyclicity implies weak mixing. No matching resolution found, 10 October 2026.
