## Q109. Let p>2, F/Q_p finite, E/F unramified quadratic, and S_n(F)={γ∈GL_n(E):γγ̄=1}. I…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** conjecture (Conjecture 1.2.4) · **Collection** 2

Let p>2, F/Q_p finite, E/F unramified quadratic, and S_n(F)={γ∈GL_n(E):γγ̄=1}. Is φ↦[x↦∂Orb(x,φ)] from the full spherical Hecke space H(S_n(F)) injective on regular-semisimple triples x=(γ,u,vᵀ)∈S_n(F)×F^n×(F^n)^∨ matching the nonsplit unitary space, for every n≥3? Here ∂Orb is the s=0 derivative of the η(det h)|det h|^(−s)-weighted GL_n(F) orbital integral with test function φ⊗1_(O_F^n×(O_F^n)^∨). The thesis proves n=2.

**Context.** Origin: The thesis explicitly proposes this large-image conjecture in contrast to the earlier large-kernel conjecture in a different setting. Its subsequent paper identifies itself as a subset of this dissertation.

**Source.** Evan Chen. *Explicit formulas for weighted orbital integrals for the inhomogeneous and semi-Lie arithmetic fundamental lemmas conjectured for the full spherical Hecke algebra*. Massachusetts Institute of Technology, 2025. Advisor(s): Wei Zhang. [primary source](https://dspace.mit.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/5f581212-ca5a-4364-bba7-ceabc195b9aa/content) · [record](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/aae651e7-f8d3-44b0-9080-41149c6292c8) Location: Conjecture 1.2.4, printed p. 32; orbital integral in Definition 8.1.1. Status evidence, checked 5 October 2026: Current arXiv text repeats it as Conjecture 1.4 and proves only n=2 (Theorem 1.9). Current author publication list and targeted searches yielded no higher-rank resolution.

## Q117. Call A⊂Z_(>1) 2-primitive if no member divides a product of one or two other dis…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 2

Call A⊂Z_(>1) 2-primitive if no member divides a product of one or two other distinct members. Let τ₂ be the infimum of λ for which Σ\_(n∈A,n≤x)n^(−λ)≤Σ\_(p≤x)p^(−λ) holds for every such A and every x≥2, where p ranges over primes. Determine τ₂ exactly; can it be characterized by a functional equation? The established interval is [0.5,0.7983].

**Context.** Origin: Original research question from Lichtman’s joint work with Tsz Ho Chan and Carl Pomerance, first formulated before filing in arXiv:2012.01677 (2020), incorporated in Chapter 10; it is not the older Erdős primitive-set conjecture. Supervisor verified on dissertation title page.

**Source.** Jared Duker Lichtman. *Topics in multiplicative number theory*. University of Oxford, 2023. Advisor(s): James Maynard. [primary source](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:61089b6c-7a44-4064-a27b-5bcaeca598a4/files/d000000766) · [record](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A61089b6c-7a44-4064-a27b-5bcaeca598a4) Location: Chapter 10, §10.1, unnumbered question immediately after (10.5), printed p. 246; definition on p. 245. Status evidence, checked 5 October 2026: The authors’ paper explicitly leaves the exact exponent open. Searches through 2026-10-05 found no exact value. The May 2026 von-Mangoldt-chain paper solves several other primitive-set conjectures but does not treat this critical exponent; July 2026 arXiv:2607.15306 concerns cardinality of strongly 2-primitive sets, not this threshold. This is a negative literature-search finding, not proof of exhaustive coverage.

**Further links.** [1](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~carlp/k-primitivev8.pdf) · [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.01677) · [3](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00301) · [4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15306)

## Q204. Let \$p\ge11\$ be prime with \$p\equiv2,5\pmod9\$, choose \$s\in\mathbb F_{p^2}\$ with…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 3

For each cube root of unity \$j\in\mathbb F_{p^2}\$, put \$u_j=\sum_{b\in\mathbb F_p,\\,1+bs\in j(\mathbb F_{p^2}^{\times})^3}\zeta^b\$.
