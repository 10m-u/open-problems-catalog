## Q4073. Does some bi-contact structure have ξ₋ tight and ξ₊ overtwisted?

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 41

Does some bi-contact structure have ξ₋ tight and ξ₊ overtwisted?

**Context.** On a closed connected oriented smooth three-manifold M, a bi-contact structure is an everywhere-transverse pair ξ₋=kerα₋, ξ₊=kerα₊ of C¹ plane fields, where α₋∧dα₋<0 and α₊∧dα₊>0. Two contact structures are isotopic if an ambient isotopy carries one to the other; bi-contact homotopy is a continuous path of such transverse pairs. A contact structure is overtwisted if an embedded disk is tangent to its planes along its boundary, and tight otherwise. Degree/advisor verification: https://math.gatech.edu/graduate-programs/dissertations https://etnyre.math.gatech.edu/professionalstuff/gradstudents.html

**Source.** Surena Hozoori. *Contact Geometric Theory of Anosov Flows in Dimension Three and Related Topics*. Georgia Institute of Technology, 2022. Advisor(s): John Etnyre. [primary source](https://etnyre.math.gatech.edu/professionalstuff/HozooriThesis.pdf) Location: Questions2.5.4/2.5.6, p.47.

## Q4174. Does 2g_f(K)≤td(K) always hold?

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 42

Does 2g_f(K)≤td(K) always hold?

**Context.** For a nontrivial smooth knot K⊂S³, let g_f(K) be the least genus of a Seifert surface with free fundamental group of its complement, and σ\_t(V_K) the difference between the largest and smallest exponents of its Jones polynomial. Define td(K) as the minimum depth over ordinary oriented binary skein trees for all diagrams of K: each vertex branches by switching one crossing and by its oriented smoothing; leaves are unlinks. Depth is the maximum root-to-leaf edge count, counting both edge types; Reidemeister moves and diagram isotopies have zero cost.

**Source.** Michał Jabłonowski. *Integer Knot Invariants: Inequalities, Computations, and Open Problems*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22652v5) Location: §5, candidates (c4) and (c7), p. 20; Appendix A.4, A.6 and A.30.

**Literature check.** Status checked 10 October 2026: Current v5 retains both comparisons. Its verified knot families do not prove the universal statements; no later resolution was found.

## Q4176. Does every such Lefschetz fibration admit a two-section?

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 42

Does every such Lefschetz fibration admit a two-section?

**Context.** Here f:X→S² is relatively minimal, X is a closed connected smooth oriented 4-manifold, regular fibers are connected, critical values are distinct, and the nonempty critical locus consists only of positive Lefschetz singularities. Relative minimality excludes fiber spheres of self-intersection −1. A two-section is a possibly disconnected embedded oriented surface disjoint from Crit(f), restricting to a degree-two simple branched cover of S² with only positive branch points.

**Source.** R. İnanç Baykur and Noriyuki Hamada. *Sections, pseudosections, and multisections of Lefschetz fibrations*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01776v1) Location: Questions 20–21, pp. 30–31.

## Q4186. If sh₀(K)≤3, must K bound a locally flat embedded disk in B⁴?

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Question 6.3) · **Collection** 42

If sh₀(K)≤3, must K bound a locally flat embedded disk in B⁴?

**Context.** For a knot K⊂S³, let X₀(K) be B⁴ with a zero-framed 2-handle attached along K, using the Seifert framing, and let C be its cocore. The topological shaking number sh₀(K) is the least geometric intersection |S∩C| over locally flat embedded spheres S generating H₂(X₀(K);Z) and transverse to C, or ∞ if none exists. No restriction is imposed on the complement's fundamental group.

**Source.** Weizhe Shen. *Topological Shaking Numbers and Cabling*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06429v1) Location: Question 6.3, p. 17; definition p. 1.

## Q4193. Is max{χ\_p(x):0≠x∈H₂(L(pn,1);Z/p)}=2−n for every odd prime p and every n≥1?

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** conjecture (Conjecture 4.22) · **Collection** 42

Is max{χ\_p(x):0≠x∈H₂(L(pn,1);Z/p)}=2−n for every odd prime p and every n≥1?

**Context.** Doctoral status: University of Sydney PhD student; primary supervisor Jonathan Spreer, auxiliary supervisor Stephan Tillmann. No completed dissertation is claimed. Let p be an odd prime, n≥1, and M=L(pn,1). A reduced weighted Z_p-folded surface is a compact embedded two-dimensional polyhedron with oriented surface sheets meeting along disjoint circles, connected complement, and positive integer sheet-weights at most p/2. At each singular arc, the incident sheets, counted with weights, number p and induce the same boundary orientation. Let Sᵘ replace each sheet by its weight's number of parallel copies, retaining its singular boundary. The resulting mod-p fundamental cycle defines [S]∈H₂(M;Z/p). Define χ\_p(x) as the maximum χ(Sᵘ) over these reduced surfaces with [S]=x.

**Source.** William Hobkirk. *Z_p-folded surfaces and the Z_p-Thurston norm*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09422v1) Location: Conjecture 4.22, p. 39; definitions pp. 7, 9–10, 24–27, 35–36.

**Literature check.** Status checked 10 October 2026: Current v1 proves the p=3 case and retains the general conjecture. No later resolution was found.
