## Q439. Adaptive query complexity of pointwise decision sufficiency

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 5

Allow successive exact queries q_tᵀc to depend on earlier answers. What is the minimum worst-case query count until the consistent cost fiber lies in one action’s optimality region? When is it below d\*? This is deployment-time branching, not offline construction of one fixed dataset.

**Context.** Attribution: Explicit restricted-complexity and adaptive-query questions in the 2026 paper.
