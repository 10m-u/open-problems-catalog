## Q4499. Characterize exactly the ordered quadruples (α\_1,α\_2,α\_3,α\_4)∈(0,∞)^4, with Σ\_jα\_j>2, which…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 45

Characterize exactly the ordered quadruples (α\_1,α\_2,α\_3,α\_4)∈(0,∞)^4, with Σ\_jα\_j>2, which occur as the angles divided by π of such a spherical quadrilateral. No conformal modulus is prescribed in this existence question. Integer and noninteger angles are both permitted.

**Context.** AP45-S22 — Geometric function theory / spherical metrics. A spherical quadrilateral is a surface homeomorphic to a closed disk, with four cyclically ordered marked boundary vertices, carrying a curvature-one Riemannian metric smooth away from those vertices. Boundary arcs between consecutive vertices are geodesic; at the vertices the metric has interior angles πα\_j, with α\_j>0, allowing removable corners when α\_j=1. There are no interior conic singularities. Its developing map to the unit sphere need not be injective.

**Source.** Alexandre Eremenko. *Possible shapes of spherical quadrilaterals,*. 2025. [primary source](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~eremenko/dvi/quadr.pdf) Location: pp. 1–2; Author-hosted question; no dissertation attribution.
