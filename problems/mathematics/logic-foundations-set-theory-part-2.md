**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Question 3.7) · **Collection** 38

Fix prime p and e∈N_{>0}∪{∞}. Take henselian (K,v,t),(L,w,s) of characteristic p and imperfection degree e, with perfect completions and 0&lt;v(t),w(s)<∞. Assume their coarsenings O_v[1/t],O_w[1/s] are separably tame. Does (vK,v(t))≡(wL,w(s)) together with O_v/(t)≡O_w/(s) imply (K,v,t)≡(L,w,s)? Here [K:K^p]=p^e; e=∞ means infinite degree. Separably tame means trivially valued, or having p-divisible value group, perfect residue field and no proper immediate separable algebraic extension; immediate preserves residue field and value group.

**Context.** Origin: Older embedding problem; new pointed-AKE question. Setup: Languages: rings with valuation-ring predicate and displayed constants; ordered groups for values, rings for quotients. O_v is the valuation ring; ≡ means elementary equivalence.

**Source.** Franziska Jahnke and Jonas van der Schaaf. *AKE principles for deeply ramified fields*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.29528v1) Location: Question 3.7, p.13.
