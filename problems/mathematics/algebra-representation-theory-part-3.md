## Q5003. Measure-equivalence classes avoiding free subgroups

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Problem 1) · **Reviewed extraction** 2026-10-13-250

Does there exist a countable discrete nonamenable group Γ such that no countable group measure-equivalent to Γ contains a subgroup isomorphic to the nonabelian free group F₂? More strongly, can Γ be chosen torsion so that every countable group measure-equivalent to Γ is torsion? Two countable groups are measure-equivalent if they admit commuting, essentially free, measure-preserving actions on a nonzero standard σ-finite measure space, each action having a measurable fundamental domain of finite positive measure. A group is torsion if every element has finite order; nonamenable means it has no left-invariant mean on bounded functions.

**Source.** Maxime Gheysens. *Representing groups against all odds*. Lausanne, EPFL, 2017. [primary source](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/6cbdac52-36ab-428d-8133-fb116acc4769/content) · [record](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/handle/20.500.14299/139743) Location: Problem1, printed p. 33; physical PDF p. 45.

**Literature check.** Reviewed 2026-10-13: Gaboriau–Lyons and Bowen–Hoff–Ioana provide measurable embeddings or free orbit relations after an extension. These results do not produce a measure-equivalent whole group containing a free subgroup. No resolution of the entire measure-equivalence-class avoidance question was located in the dated primary scan.

## Q5004. Compactness from affine fixed points on Krein spaces

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Problem 2) · **Reviewed extraction** 2026-10-13-250

Let G be a Hausdorff topological group. Suppose every action of G by continuous affine automorphisms on any Hausdorff locally convex space E has a common fixed point whenever E has the Krein property and at least one orbit map g↦g·x is continuous. Must G be compact? The Krein property means that the closed convex hull of every compact subset of E is compact. No joint continuity of the action is required.

**Source.** Maxime Gheysens. *Representing groups against all odds*. Lausanne, EPFL, 2017. [primary source](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/6cbdac52-36ab-428d-8133-fb116acc4769/content) · [record](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/handle/20.500.14299/139743) Location: Problem2, printed p. 105; physical PDF p. 117.

**Literature check.** Reviewed 2026-10-13: The source proves compactness implies this fixed-point property, which implies precompactness; complete groups and groups measurable in their completion have positive results. The current primary scan found no extension covering every Hausdorff group, including incomplete groups.

## Q5006. Closed subgroups of locally compact unitarizable groups

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Problem 11) · **Reviewed extraction** 2026-10-13-250

Call a Hausdorff topological group G unitarizable if every strongly continuous representation π:G→GL(H) on a complex Hilbert space with sup_g‖π(g)‖<∞ admits a bounded invertible S such that S⁻¹π(g)S is unitary for every g. If G is locally compact and unitarizable, must every closed subgroup of G, with its induced topology, be unitarizable? In particular, must G contain no subgroup isomorphic to F₂ that is discrete in the induced topology?

**Source.** Maxime Gheysens. *Representing groups against all odds*. Lausanne, EPFL, 2017. Subgroup stability is already known for discrete groups. A current discrete Dixmier closure lead does not establish the corresponding result for all locally compact groups; no such full result was located.

## Q5007. Kazhdan and unitarizable locally compact groups

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Problem 12) · **Reviewed extraction** 2026-10-13-250

Call a Hausdorff topological group G unitarizable if every strongly continuous representation π:G→GL(H) on a complex Hilbert space with sup_g‖π(g)‖<∞ admits a bounded invertible S such that S⁻¹π(g)S is unitary for every g. If G is locally compact, unitarizable and has Kazhdan property (T), must it be compact? Property (T) means that every strongly continuous unitary representation π on a Hilbert space having almost invariant unit vectors has a nonzero invariant vector; almost invariance means that for every compact K⊂G and ε>0 there is a unit vector v with sup_{g∈K}‖π(g)v−v‖<ε.

A discrete-group closure claim does not automatically cover the source’s continuous locally compact representations.

## Q5038. Finite-index maximal subgroups in branch groups

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** stated as open · **Reviewed extraction** 2026-10-13-250

For d≥2 let T_d be the rooted tree of all finite words in {1,…,d}, with an edge from w to wi. A subgroup G≤Aut(T_d) acts faithfully. For a vertex v let rist_G(v) consist of automorphisms that fix every vertex outside the subtree beginning with v; let rist_G(m) be the subgroup generated by rist_G(v) for all length-m vertices. ## Q5040. Exponential growth with every maximal subgroup normal

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** stated as open · **Reviewed extraction** 2026-10-13-250

For a finite symmetric generating set S of a group G let b_{G,S}(r) be the number of group elements expressible as a product of at most r members of S. Exponential growth means lim_{r→∞}b_{G,S}(r)^{1/r}>1; intermediate growth means b_{G,S}(r)/r^k→∞ for every k≥1 while lim_{r→∞}b_{G,S}(r)^{1/r}=1. These growth types are independent of the finite generating set. Does there exist a finitely generated group G of exponential growth for which every maximal proper subgroup M&lt;G is normal in G? Here maximal means no subgroup lies strictly between M and G; all maximal subgroups are quantified, including those of infinite index.

**Source.** Aglaia Myropolska. *On AutFn action on group presentations*. Université de Genève, 2015. ## Q5046. Automaticity of CAT(0) groups

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** stated as open · **Reviewed extraction** 2026-10-13-250

An isometric action of a discrete group G on a metric space X is proper here if, for one (equivalently every) x∈X and each R<∞, the set {g∈G:d(x,gx)≤R} is finite. It is cocompact if some compact K⊂X satisfies GK=X. A CAT(0) group admits a proper cocompact isometric action on a CAT(0) geodesic metric space, meaning geodesic triangles are no thicker than their Euclidean comparison triangles. Does there exist a CAT(0) group that is not automatic? Automatic means that for some finite symmetric generating set S there is a regular language L⊂S\* giving one representative for every group element, and a constant K, such that whenever u,v∈L represent g and gs with s∈S, their paths from the identity in the Cayley graph stay at distance at most K at every equal integer time, with a finished path held at its endpoint.

**Source.** Katarzyna Jankiewicz. *Cubical dimension and obstructions to actions on CAT(0) cube complexes*. McGill University, 2018. [primary source](https://mcgill.scholaris.ca/server/api/core/bitstreams/0145dbf0-b91b-4d5f-a338-5a20e57df291/content) · [record](https://mcgill.scholaris.ca/handle/20.500.14905/136998) Location: Unlabelled automaticity clause in Section 1.11.2, printed p. 16; physical PDF p. 17.

**Literature check.** Reviewed 2026-10-13: Leary–Minasyan construct nonbiautomatic CAT(0) groups but Section 12 leaves their automaticity open. Current 2025 higher-rank GBS work characterizes biautomaticity, not automaticity. No complete nonautomatic CAT(0) example or theorem that all are automatic was verified in the dated bounded primary scan.

## Q5047. Virtual cocompact cubulation of Coxeter groups

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** stated as open · **Reviewed extraction** 2026-10-13-250

A CAT(0) cube complex is a simply connected complex of Euclidean unit cubes glued along faces, with flag vertex links and the induced Euclidean path metric. An isometric action of a discrete group G on a metric space X is proper here if, for one (equivalently every) x∈X and each R<∞, the set {g∈G:d(x,gx)≤R} is finite. It is cocompact if some compact K⊂X satisfies GK=X. For every finite set S and symmetric matrix (m_st) with m_ss=1 and m_st∈{2,3,…,∞} for s≠t, let W=⟨S | s²=1 for s∈S, (st)^(m_st)=1 whenever s≠t and m_st<∞⟩. ## Q5048. Classification of properly cubulated Artin groups

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Question 6.6.6) · **Reviewed extraction** 2026-10-13-250

A CAT(0) cube complex is a simply connected complex of Euclidean unit cubes glued along faces, with flag vertex links and the induced Euclidean path metric. An isometric action of a discrete group G on a metric space X is proper here if, for one (equivalently every) x∈X and each R<∞, the set {g∈G:d(x,gx)≤R} is finite. It is cocompact if some compact K⊂X satisfies GK=X. For a finite simple graph Γ with edges labelled by integers m_st≥2, define A_Γ=⟨VΓ | alternating(s,t,m_st)=alternating(t,s,m_st) for each edge {s,t}⟩, where alternating(s,t,m) is the length-m word starting with s and alternating s,t. Classify the labelled graphs Γ for which A_Γ admits a proper action by cubical automorphisms on a finite-dimensional CAT(0) cube complex. In particular, determine this for every braid group B_n, n≥2, with generators σ\_1,…,σ\_(n−1), relations σ\_iσ\_(i+1)σ\_i=σ\_(i+1)σ\_iσ\_(i+1) and σ\_iσ\_j=σ\_jσ\_i for |i−j|≥2.
