## Q279. Given \$M\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}\$ with \$\\|M\\|\_2\le1\$ and fixed \$C>0\$, can one ef…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem (Problem 4) · **Collection** 3

Given \$M\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}\$ with \$\\|M\\|\_2\le1\$ and fixed \$C>0\$, can one efficiently construct a perturbation \$N\$ with \$\\|N\\|\_2\le n^{-C}\$ and \$O(n\log n)\$ nonzero entries such that both \$\log\kappa_V(M+N)\$ and \$\log(1/\eta(M+N))\$ are bounded by polynomials in \$\log n\$? Here \$\kappa_V(A)=\inf_{A=VDV^{-1}}\\|V\\|\_2\\|V^{-1}\\|\_2\$, with \$D\$ diagonal, and \$\eta(A)=\min_{i\ne j}|\lambda_i(A)-\lambda_j(A)|\$. Randomized constructions are allowed, with the desired bounds holding with high probability.

**Context.** Origin: The thesis asks this sparsity improvement of joint work with Srivastava and Zeng.

**Source.** Rikhav Shah. *Hermitian Eigenproblems and Regularization of Nonnormal Eigenproblems*. University of California, Berkeley, 2025. Advisor(s): Nikhil Srivastava. [primary source](https://escholarship.org/content/qt2750t20z/qt2750t20z.pdf) · [record](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2750t20z) Location: Section 1.6, Problem 4 (sparser-perturbation part), printed p. 16; definitions in Chapter 4, p. 69.
