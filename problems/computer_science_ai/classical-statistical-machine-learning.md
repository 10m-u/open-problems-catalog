## Q68. For finite simplices and V(x,y)=x^(T)Ry/(x^(T)Sy), with R real and S nonnegative…

**Status:** Open, partial results · **Kind:** open problem (Problem 13.4.2) · **Collection** 1

For finite simplices and V(x,y)=x^(T)Ry/(x^(T)Sy), with R real and S nonnegative satisfying x^(T)Sy≥ζ>0 everywhere, does projected extragradient with a suitable constant step size converge in last iterate to a Nash equilibrium?

**Context.** Origin: The ratio-game question is joint with Daskalakis and Foster; the partially observable decision-process questions are joint with Moitra and Rohatgi.

**Source.** Noah Golowich. *Theoretical Foundations for Learning in Games and Dynamic Environments*. Massachusetts Institute of Technology, 2025. Advisor(s): Constantinos Daskalakis; Ankur Moitra. [primary source](https://dspace.mit.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/209ed58a-6a99-4877-8ddd-db5b48ab822a/content) · [record](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/093f570f-8544-468e-bd28-4d9be1819bda) Location: Problem 13.4.2, p.586; Eq. (13.12), p.585. Search-qualified status: The 2025 thesis retains it and §13.9 (p.609) distinguishes later last-iterate results for more complicated updates. Targeted ratio-game/extragradient searches found no resolution for the stated method.

**Results.**

- **Partial result**: For 2x2 ratio games the gradient descent-ascent field is D^-2 J grad H with separable H=A(p)+B(q) (cubic A, B from the pencil (N,D)); every interior equilibrium is a centre; all closed orbits are convex (normalisation to P g(xi)+R g(zeta), g=t^2/2-t^3/3, and the identity xi^2(3-2zeta)(1-2zeta)-(1-zeta)^2(4xi^2-1)=(1-zeta)^2-xi^2 with smoothstep symmetry); hence H is a Lyapunov function for the exact extragradient map and EG with small constant step converges linearly from every compact sub-level set of the closed-orbit region. Boundary equilibria, starts outside that region and games larger than 2x2 remain open. No prior treatment found in a bounded search. [Details](../../solutions/catalog-research/round3.md).

## Q292. Is U(1,r)=r?

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 3

Is U(1,r)=r?

**Context.** Origin: MDL questions concern the thesis’s well-specified uniform bounds. Profile posterior is joint with Ohannessian and Srebro, preprinted in March 2026. Shared setup for questions 292, 293: Observe m independent identically distributed binary-labelled examples. L_S and L_D denote empirical and population classification error. A countable classifier class has prior pi. MDL_lambda minimizes lambda times −log_2 pi(h) plus log_2 binomial(m,m L_S(h)), subject to L_S(h) ≤ 1/2. In the well-specified model Y is h_star(X) flipped independently with probability r, where 0 < r < 1/2 and pi(h_star) ≥ 0.1. Define U(lambda,r) as the large-m limit of the supremum, over such instances, of expected population error.

**Source.** Xiaohan Zhu. *Overfitting and Generalizing with MDL and (PAC) Bayesian Learning in Supervised Classification*. University of Chicago, 2026. Advisor(s): Nathan Srebro.
