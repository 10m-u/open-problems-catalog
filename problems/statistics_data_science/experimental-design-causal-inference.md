## Q401. Polynomial-time recognition of rationally identifiable mixed graphs

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 5

Given an acyclic mixed graph \$G=(V,D,B)\$, constrain \$\Lambda\$ to directed edges and positive-definite \$\Omega\$ to bidirected edges off the diagonal. Is there a polynomial-time necessary-and-sufficient test for rational identifiability from \$\Sigma=(I-\Lambda)^{-T}\Omega(I-\Lambda)^{-1}\$? Rational identifiability means a rational inverse outside a proper algebraic exceptional set. Otherwise, establish the corresponding hardness under stated complexity assumptions.

**Context.** Doctoral researcher: Nils B. Sturma. Technical University of Munich, 2024. Primary advisor: Mathias Drton. Attribution: A longstanding identification question, explicitly restated in Sturma’s doctoral thesis.

**Source.** Nils B. Sturma. *Identifiability and Statistical Inference in Latent Variable Modeling*. 2024. Advisor(s): Mathias Drton. [primary source](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/doc/1745362/y6x2mnkamj6nkmyxk0zkpiwq7.PhD_Thesis.pdf) · [record](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/1745362) Location: Chapter 5, printed p. 25 (PDF p. 30).

**Literature check.** Status, checked 5 October 2026: Hollering–Misra–Sturma 2026 still calls complete characterization open. Its degree-bounded algorithm does not yield unrestricted polynomial-time recognition.

**Further links.** [1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20516) · [2](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.20516v1) · [3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12528)

## Q402. How large must recursive identifying formulas become?

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 5

For a rationally identifiable acyclic mixed graph G, order its free parameters θ. For each parameter q require an identity q a(θ\_&lt;q,Σ)−b(θ\_&lt;q,Σ)=0 on the covariance model, with a not identically zero there. Define ID_G as the minimum, over valid orders and such identities, of the maximum total polynomial degree. Determine the growth of ID_G with graph size and structure; in particular, is it bounded uniformly, or what unbounded growth is unavoidable?

**Context.** Attribution: The conclusion explicitly identifies growth of the maximal degree of identifying formulas as its most important future problem.

**Source.** Benjamin Hollering; Pratik Misra; Nils Sturma. *Efficient Symbolic Computations for Identifying Causal Effects*. 2026. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20516) Location: Definitions 2.8–2.10; Remark 5.3; §8 conclusion, pp. 16–17.

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 5

## Q406. Complete identification for hierarchical causal models

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 5

Interventions are hard settings of unit variables or soft kernels on subunit variables conditional on subunit parents. ## Q415. Real-algebraic hardness of generic causal-parameter identification

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 5

Given an acyclic mixed graph G, define φ\_G(Λ,Ω)=(I−Λ)^(−T)Ω(I−Λ)^(−1), with graph-compatible real Λ and positive-definite graph-compatible Ω. Generic identification asks whether φ\_G has singleton fibers for Zariski-almost-all admissible parameters. Is this decision problem hard for ∃R or for ∀R under polynomial-time reductions? Can its known ∃∀R∩∀∃R upper bound be lowered to ∃R or ∀R? Here ∃R and ∀R are existential-real feasibility and its complement.

**Context.** Attribution: The conclusion explicitly conjectures hardness for one of the two one-alternation real complexity classes and asks whether the upper bounds can be improved to them.

**Source.** Julian Dörfler; Benito van der Zander; Markus Bläser; Maciej Liśkiewicz. *On the Complexity of Identification in Linear Structural Causal Models*. 2024. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12528) Location: §8 conclusion; §2.1 generic-fiber definition.

**Literature check.** Status, checked 5 October 2026: The 2024 real-complexity bounds remain unresolved in later searches; the 2026 degree-bounded rational method answers a different restricted target.

## Q441. Gap-free square-root regret for unknown binary causal graphs

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 5

In an unknown fully observed DAG with n binary nonreward variables and binary reward Y, let X₁≡1 and P(X=1|pa(X))=θ\_Xᵀpa(X)+ε\_X, with unknown nonnegative θ\_X and centered sub-Gaussian noise restricted to keep every probability in [0,1]. Each round intervenes from a fixed combinatorial menu containing the null and all atomic interventions, and observes all variables. Can expected regret against the best intervention be poly(n)√T polylog(T), without a positive lower bound on nonzero coefficients? Oracle access is allowed to joint maximization of expected intervened reward over actions and parameters in current confidence ellipsoids; implementing that oracle is not charged.

**Context.** Attribution: The paper obtains O(n^3 T^(2/3) log T) without the edge-weight gap and explicitly asks for square-root-in-T regret without that assumption.

**Source.** Shi Feng; Neng Xiong; Wei Chen. *Combinatorial Causal Bandits without Graph Skeleton*. 2025. [primary source](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v260/feng25a.html) Location: §6, Theorem 9 and following paragraph; §7; model §3.

**Source.** Clément Yvernes; Emilie Devijver; Marianne Clausel; Eric Gaussier. *Identifiability in Causal Abstractions: A Hierarchy of Criteria*. 2025. [primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06213) Location: Definitions 2 and 4; §6 “IG vs ICD conjecture”.
