## Q79. Is PRM=2 for the thesis’s bipartite trading-network model? A platform adds missi…

**Status:** Open · **Kind:** open problem · **Collection** 1

Is PRM=2 for the thesis’s bipartite trading-network model? A platform adds missing buyer–seller edges; unit-demand buyers and unit-supply sellers trade at seller-optimal competitive prices, with platform-favorable tie-breaking. Revenue is the total price on added-edge trades. PRM is the supremum of optimal achievable welfare divided by welfare under any revenue-maximizing added-edge configuration.

**Context.** Origin: This conjecture is joint with Luca D’Amico-Wong, Yannai Gonczarowski and David Parkes, in work incorporated into Chapter 4.

**Source.** Gary Qiurui Ma. *Pricing, Matching, and Bundling: an Equilibrium Analysis of Online Platforms*. Harvard University, 2026. Advisor(s): David C. Parkes; Yannai A. Gonczarowski.
