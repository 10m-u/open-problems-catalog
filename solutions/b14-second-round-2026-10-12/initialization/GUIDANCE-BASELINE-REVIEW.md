# Independent review of the guidance baseline

**Verdict:** the four-policy held-out experiment has valid original-cost certification, a consistent common nominal elimination budget, and properly amortized training cost. The timing-coverage issue found during review was corrected with an outer query timer. The bounded comparison does not establish a practically meaningful learned-policy advantage, and P1/P6's broader ambitions remain active.

I read [experiment.py](../guidance/experiment.py), [RESULT.md](../guidance/RESULT.md), the retained experiment and all 160 packets. The four training inputs are Euclidean n=8,10 with seeds 0,1; the twenty test identities have disjoint seeds, include wholly held-out families, and include n=12 absent from training. There are 192 examples and forty matched instance/repetition pairs per policy. Features depend on the feasible incumbent and already evaluated root states, without using the test optimum or future reference-DP work. The learned model chooses only where to spend bound computations; it does not justify deletions.

Both ordinary and learned policies initialize all four roots before further selection. Random seeds and rotating execution order are explicit. Each run spends the same nominal 0.05 seconds on elimination/inference, except that learned runs debit training_seconds/40. Summing the forty debits charges the entire training batch once. This is batch amortization; the report correctly requires a full training charge for a single-query deployment. The last snapshot can overrun, and those actual overruns are retained. No identical-actual-time or statistical-significance claim is warranted.

The original implementation called the sum of incumbent, elimination, verification and exact-stage timers total proof time, excluding setup and packet construction. I reported this to the parent. The corrected implementation starts an outer timer before setup, records `query_wall_seconds` after certification, exact answer checks and residual-structure measurements, and adds the training debit as `total_proof_seconds`. The old four-stage sum remains separately labeled. Artifact serialization and the independent full-DP control campaign remain outside the time to an already checked answer; they must not be described as included archival/export work.

[check_programme_reviews.py](check_programme_reviews.py) re-verifies all 160 original-cost packets, recosts every answer, validates residual degrees and the reported decomposition-width order, and computes twenty full complete-metric optima with an independently implemented subset DP. Every answer agrees. It checks forty queries per policy, matched identities, summary counts, overrun arithmetic, total training debit, and the corrected outer/stage timing identities. [Receipt](PROGRAMME-REVIEW-CHECK-RESULTS.json). Retained certificate counts can change on a rerun because the wall budget changes how many snapshots finish; exact answers and certification remain invariant.

This is a correctness/protocol audit of the baseline, not a replication of wall-clock performance or a claim that the chosen ridge learner, four roots, feature set, or budget is optimal. Further learned objectives are separate actual attempts; this baseline alone does not complete P6's broader speed-improvement question.
