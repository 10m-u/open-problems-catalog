# Sources and attribution

Checked primary material on 13 October 2026:

**Tomasz Kościuszko, Arithmetic Progressions in Midpoint Colourings,
arXiv:2610.11847v1 (2026).**
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11847v1
Problem 4 supplies the finite-field extremal target; Theorem 3 gives the
published-preprint quadratic codimension bound. It is not the source of
this continuation's occupied-fibre, 16-point, or all-dimension plane-free
results. This continuation does not improve the earlier package's general
O(k log k) bound.

**Rupert Li, The Entropic Sum-Product Phenomenon,
arXiv:2607.29042v1 (2026).**
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.29042v1
Section 8 states the sum–difference inequality with coefficient 2 and asks
about its sharpness. Lemma 7.5 contains a finite-entropy truncation argument.
Conjecture 1.10 is the different sum–product conjecture, not Q4660's
sum–difference sharpness statement. The catalog label is preserved but
its correction is carried forward in PROVENANCE.json.

**A. E. Brouwer and A. Schrijver, The blocking number of an affine space,
Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A 24 (1978), 251–253.**
https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/9858/9858D.pdf
The primary PDF states the exact hyperplane-blocking number k(q−1)+1.
Its first page was visually checked as well as its parsed text. The
manuscript's polynomial blocking argument is an attributed reproduction
of this classical method, not a new result.

**R. E. Jamison, Covering finite fields with cosets of subspaces,
Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A 22 (1977), 253–266.**
https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(77)90001-2
Independent classical source for the affine blocking theorem. The
Brouwer–Schrijver primary paper also explicitly attributes Jamison's result.

## Supplied internal predecessors

`B2-new-results-2026-10-13.zip` establishes the preceding general midpoint
O(k log k) argument. `B2-further-results-2026-10-13.zip` establishes the
cap classification, full-fibre inheritance, finite support-boundary
obstructions, and the current certified entropy lower bound.

The cap classification is reproved in the appendix, but it is not counted
as new in this continuation. Full-fibre inheritance is used with attribution.
The new occupied-fibre statement is distinct and needs the new fibre
normal form. Standard truncation, integer modelling, and finite-mixture
entropy estimates are not each claimed as new; the combined prescribed-tail
supremum and stationarity consequences are the scoped additions.

These checks do not establish worldwide priority or the absence of other
relevant literature. No external peer review has occurred.
