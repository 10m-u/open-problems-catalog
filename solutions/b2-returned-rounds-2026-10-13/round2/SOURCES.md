# Sources and scope — checked 13 October 2026

## Primary statements

**Tomasz Kościuszko, Arithmetic Progressions in Midpoint Colourings**, arXiv:2610.11847v1, Problem 4 and Theorem 3.
https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11847v1

Problem 4 is the midpoint-partition extremal problem over F_3. The source's Theorem 3 has codimension O(k^2). The preceding local continuation improved this to O(k log k). The present cap classification, full-fibre consequences, and k+2 obstruction are proved in the new manuscript, not attributed to this paper.

**Rupert Li, The Entropic Sum-Product Phenomenon**, arXiv:2607.29042v1, Conjecture 1.10 and Section 8.
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.29042v1

Important location correction: Conjecture 1.10 states the asymptotic sum–product inequality 2H(X+X')+H(XX') >= (4-o(1))H(X). Section 8 separately records the sum–difference inequality with coefficient 2 and says sharpness is unknown. Q4660's mathematical statement agrees with this latter question; its catalog label conflates the locations. No claim to solve Conjecture 1.10 is made.

**Ashok Vardhan Makkuva and Yihong Wu, Equivalence of additive-combinatorial linear inequalities for Shannon entropy and differential entropy**, arXiv:1601.07498v2, Proposition 1 and Remark 2.
https://arxiv.org/html/1601.07498v2

The optimal sum–difference ratio is dimension-independent, and the paper states the existing upper bound 2. Independent tensor powers preserve a fixed ratio. No new priority claim is made for tensorization or digit encodings.

**W. T. Gowers, Ben Green, Freddie Manners, and Terence Tao, Marton's conjecture in abelian groups with bounded torsion**, arXiv:2404.02244v2, Theorem 1.1.
https://arxiv.org/html/2404.02244v2

This validates the covering ingredient used in the preceding local general codimension theorem: a small-doubling finite set in exponent-p groups is covered by polynomially many cosets of a subgroup no larger than the set. The new two-colour classification does not require PFR.
