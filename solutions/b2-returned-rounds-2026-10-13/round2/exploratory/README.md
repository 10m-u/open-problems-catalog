# Exploratory records — not proof certificates

These files preserve the search routes that suggested or tested the new results. NumPy/SciPy are optional dependencies for numerical and MILP scripts; the three main verification programs do not use them.

The 43-node two-colour cap obstruction search suggested the classification. The manuscript now proves it by elementary incidence counting, independently of that search. Correlated-grid and alternate-support entropy searches were inconclusive. Stationarity equations resolved very small tail probabilities and suggested the twelve-point rational law; their residuals are not global optimality evidence. MILP records are solver outputs, including time-limited runs, not rigorous all-dimension bounds. SPANNING-SEARCH-3.json suggested an overstrong affine-span strategy's counterexample, rechecked by the exact main verifier.

Paths in the optional scripts were made relative to their own location for portability. Time-limited numerical runs can return different exploratory records on another machine. Nothing in the new proofs or exact certificate depends on reproducing those numerical outputs.
