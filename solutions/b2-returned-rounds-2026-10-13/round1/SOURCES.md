# Primary sources and comparison scope

Checked on 13 October 2026.

## Midpoint problem and previous bound

Tomasz Kościuszko, *Arithmetic Progressions in Midpoint Colourings*,
arXiv:2610.11847v1, submitted 8 October 2026.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11847
- https://arxiv.org/html/2610.11847v1

Problem 4 is the finite-field target. Lemma 1 supplies the p=3 Fourier
increment. Theorem 3 proves codimension O(k^2) at remainder density 1/10.
The manuscript's general covering-to-avoidance argument, logarithmic bound,
and explicit density bookkeeping are new relative to that statement and
the supplied B2 attempt packet. The source already notes a linear-loss
construction direction; the block construction is an explicit companion,
not a priority claim for the necessity of linear loss.

## Established external input

W. T. Gowers, Ben Green, Freddie Manners, and Terence Tao,
*Marton's conjecture in abelian groups with bounded torsion*,
arXiv:2404.02244v2, 20 May 2024.

- https://arxiv.org/html/2404.02244v2
- https://doi.org/10.5802/afst.1839

Theorem 1.1: in an abelian group of torsion m, a nonempty finite A with
|A+A| <= K|A| can be covered by at most (2K)^{O(m^3)} cosets of a subgroup
H with |H| <= |A|. For F_p^n, every such subgroup is an F_p-linear subspace.
The note invokes this established covering theorem, not a conjectural
polynomial Bogolyubov result.

## Entropy question

Rupert Li, *The Entropic Sum-Product Phenomenon*, arXiv:2607.29042v1,
submitted 31 July 2026.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.29042
- https://arxiv.org/html/2607.29042v1

Section 8 states the entropic sum–difference upper constant two and asks
whether it is tight. The new certificate improves the uploaded B2 witness.
It is neither a global optimization result nor an exhaustive comparison
against published lower-bound examples.
