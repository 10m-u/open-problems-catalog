# Q294 — Consistency of the support-truncated profile posterior

**Proposed affirmative argument; reconstructed research checkpoint, 2026-10-10.** Original checker programs and separate reviews are not included. This note is not a peer-review or novelty certification; see [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md).

## Source and scope

The earlier investigation used Xiaohan Zhu, *Overfitting and Generalizing with MDL and (PAC) Bayesian Learning in Supervised Classification*, University of Chicago, 2026, §3.5 equations (3.14)–(3.16), and §3.6, printed p. 66: [record](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/records/kre10-b0z32), [thesis PDF](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/records/kre10-b0z32/files/PhD_Dissertation_XiaohanZhu_Final.pdf).

The thesis/catalog formula restricts posterior **support** to classifiers with empirical error at most one half. The earlier [joint preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.22644v1) displays a mean-error constraint and an untruncated Gibbs density; these are different formulations. The proof below applies only to the support-truncated formula. It neither establishes the separate integrated-Bayesian comparison nor determines a fixed-temperature worst-case curve. References and the formulation comparison are carried forward from the earlier source inspection.

## Setup and finite-sample bound

Let the classifier evaluation map be jointly measurable. Let `S` contain `m` independent observations, let `ell(h)=L_D(h)` and `ellhat_S(h)=L_S(h)`, and let `Pi` be a data-independent prior. Use natural entropy

\[
\mathsf h(u)=-u\log u-(1-u)\log(1-u),\qquad 0\log0=0.
\]

For `lambda>0`, put `t=m/lambda` and define

\[
Q_S(dh)=Z_S^{-1}\mathbf1_{\{\widehat\ell_S(h)\le1/2\}}
 e^{-t\mathsf h(\widehat\ell_S(h))}\Pi(dh). \tag{1}
\]

This agrees with the base-two formula when the binary entropy is measured in bits. If `Z_S=0`, use any measurable probability-valued fallback; the bound controls that event.

Suppose a data-independent comparator satisfies

\[
Q_*\ll\Pi,\qquad \int\ell\,dQ_*\le r<1/2,\qquad
D_{\rm KL}(Q_*\Vert\Pi)\le K<\infty, \tag{2}
\]

with KL in natural units. A bound of ten bits corresponds to `K=10 log 2`; `K=10` covers either convention.

Fix `0<epsilon<(1/2-r)/4` and set

\[
a_0=\exp\!\left[-(K+\log2)\frac{r+\varepsilon}{\varepsilon}\right],
\qquad
g=\mathsf h(r+3\varepsilon)-\mathsf h(r+2\varepsilon)>0.
\]

The proposed finite-sample bound is

\[
\mathbb E L_D(Q_S)\le r+4\varepsilon+2e^{-2m\varepsilon^2}
+\frac2{a_0}\left[e^{-tg}
+e^{t\mathsf h(r+2\varepsilon)-2m\varepsilon^2}\right]. \tag{3}
\]

The right side may be capped at one, and `Pr(Z_S=0)<=2 exp(-2m epsilon^2)`.

## Proof

Put `A={h:ell(h)<=r+epsilon}`, `a=Pi(A)` and `q=Q_*(A)`. Markov's inequality gives `q>=epsilon/(r+epsilon)>0`. Data processing of relative entropy to the indicator of `A` gives

\[
K\ge q\log\frac qa+(1-q)\log\frac{1-q}{1-a}
\ge q\log(1/a)-\log2.
\]

Absolute continuity excludes `a=0`; the case `a=1` is immediate. Consequently

\[
a\ge e^{-(K+\log2)/q}\ge a_0. \tag{4}
\]

For fixed `h` in `A`, Hoeffding's inequality bounds the probability that `ellhat_S(h)>r+2epsilon` by `exp(-2m epsilon^2)`. Integrating with respect to the prior and then applying Markov shows that the event

\[
E=\left\{\Pi\{h\in A:\widehat\ell_S(h)>r+2\varepsilon\}\le a/2\right\}
\]

has probability at least `1-2 exp(-2m epsilon^2)`. On `E`, at least `a/2` prior mass has an allowed empirical error at most `r+2epsilon`. Entropy is increasing on `[0,1/2]`, so

\[
Z_S\ge(a/2)e^{-t\mathsf h(r+2\varepsilon)}>0. \tag{5}
\]

This also bounds the probability of a zero normalizer.

Let `B={h:ell(h)>r+4epsilon}`. Split its weighted numerator between empirical errors at least `r+3epsilon` and those below that value. On the first part, every nonzero weight is at most `exp(-t h(r+3epsilon))`; the support restriction is essential here. Let

\[
R_S=\Pi\{h\in B:\widehat\ell_S(h)<r+3\varepsilon\}.
\]

Every classifier counted by `R_S` underestimates its true error by more than `epsilon`. Hoeffding and Tonelli yield `E R_S<=exp(-2m epsilon^2)`. All weights are at most one. Thus on `E`, (5) gives

\[
Q_S(B)\le\frac2a\left[e^{-tg}+e^{t\mathsf h(r+2\varepsilon)}R_S\right].
\]

On the complement of `E`, bound this mass by one. Taking expectations, using (4), and using `L_D(Q_S)<=r+4epsilon+Q_S(B)` proves (3).

## Uniform consistency

If

\[
\lambda_m\longrightarrow\infty,\qquad \lambda_m=o(m), \tag{6}
\]

then `t_m=m/lambda_m` tends to infinity while `t_m/m` tends to zero. For each fixed admissible `epsilon`, every exponential remainder in (3) tends to zero. All constants depend only on the fixed `r,K,epsilon`, not on the distribution, prior, or hypothesis space. Therefore

\[
\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{(D,\Pi,Q_*)\text{ satisfying (2)}}
\mathbb E L_D(Q_S)\le r+4\varepsilon.
\]

Letting `epsilon` decrease to zero gives the proposed uniform consistency conclusion. The catalog assumption `lambda_m log(m)/m -> 0` implies `lambda_m=o(m)`, so (6) removes that logarithmic restriction. This is uniform for **fixed** `r<1/2` and finite `K`, not a uniform rate as `r` approaches one half. Instance sequences are allowed provided each prior and comparator is independent of its sample.

## Why truncation cannot be omitted

Take a singleton input space, `Y~Bernoulli(r)` with `0<r<1/2`, and constant classifiers `h_0=0`, `h_1=1` with equal prior mass. Their empirical errors sum to one, so their empirical entropies agree for every sample. The untruncated entropy Gibbs posterior is always the prior and has population risk one half, despite the comparator `delta_{h_0}` having risk `r` and KL `log 2`.

The same prior minimizes the entropy-plus-KL objective under the weaker constraint `L_S(Q)<=1/2`: its empirical mean error is exactly one half, and its KL is zero. Thus that weaker formulation is not covered by the positive result.

For the truncated posterior, with `N~Binomial(m,r)`, the posterior selects `h_0` below `m/2`, `h_1` above it, and equal masses at a tie. Its exact expected risk is

\[
r+(1-2r)\left[\Pr(N>m/2)+\tfrac12\Pr(N=m/2)\right].
\]

The earlier work describes exact finite-model checks of this control. Those programs and certificates are unavailable in this checkpoint and have not been rerun. Review must preserve the support restriction and the fixed-comparator-risk qualification before any catalog integration.
