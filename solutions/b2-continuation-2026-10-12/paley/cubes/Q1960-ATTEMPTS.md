# Q1960: general cyclic projective cubes

12 October 2026. Full question: for every d≥2 and d|N, does a projective-d-cube-free A⊆Z/NZ have |A|≤(d−1)N/d? Repeated generators and coincident subset sums are permitted. No solution or counterexample to that full question is claimed here.

Primary source read: [Meng, On Cube-Free Problems](https://doi.org/10.37236/14052), local `texts/papers/collections-077f96b30fab35e0.txt.gz`, Sections 1–4. Its Theorem 5 proves d=3, smallest-prime-factor d, and prime-power N. Those cases are prior results. A separate [published research note](https://gist.github.com/hypnopump/8a369af584796b25c9af586b4fe39385) supplies the diagonal obstruction below; we independently check its finite witness and scope, without a novelty claim.

## A1 — Complete Meng's diagonal-progression reduction

Meng's Conjecture 10 would be sufficient: every set avoiding {x,2x,…,(d−1)x} has density at most (d−1)/d. If A contains that progression, cube freeness applied to d−1 copies of x and one y implies the d translates A,A−x,…,A−(d−1)x have empty intersection. Their complements then cover the group, proving the desired bound. This is an exact proposed proof route, but its sufficient conjecture is false even if x=0 is included.

Take d=5,N=40 and B={0,4,12,20,24,28,36}=4C, C={0,1,3,5,6,7,9}⊆Z/10Z. The set A=Z/40Z\B has size33>32 and avoids every four-term multiplier set.

To see this without search, if x is odd, 4x∈B; if x=2u with u odd, 2x=4u∈B. If x=4u, then x∈B unless u mod10 belongs to {2,4,8}; in those cases 3x,4x,2x respectively belong to B, because the corresponding multiple of u equals6 mod10. The case x=0 is covered by 0∈B. Thus some kx with 1≤k≤4 lies outside A for every x.

For every q≥1 pull back A under Z/(40q)Z→Z/40Z. It has size33q>32q and the same multiplier avoidance, including x in the reduction's kernel. This disproves the diagonal sufficient lemma for an infinite family of cyclic groups at fixed d=5.

It does **not** disprove Q1960: A contains the projective 5-cube generated by (1,1,1,5,8). Its nonempty subset sums are exactly {1,2,3,5,6,7,8,9,10,11,13,14,15,16}, all lying in A. The stronger mixed-generator restriction cannot be discarded. **One failed full-target proof route**, with an exact obstruction and a surviving full-scope gap. Numerical searches or different multiplier moments of this same witness are not additional attempts.

## A2 — Extend the prime-power theorem by CRT fibers

Use the prime-power result on each fiber of the CRT projection, then average the fiber bounds. Its needed preservation assertion is false, because a nonzero fiber is a coset rather than a subgroup and translating it to the subgroup changes every subset sum by a different multiple of the translation. In Z/12Z with d=4, the whole fiber A={1,4,7,10} over1 mod3 is 4-cube-free: among any four generators in it, three have sum0 mod3, outside A. Identifying this fiber with Z/4Z by t↦1+3t gives the entire subgroup, which contains the cube generated by four0's. Thus even a full coset can be globally cube-free while its translated fiber is not.

Projection has a separate but related defect: A={x∈Z/10Z:x mod5≠0} is 5-cube-free by the source's zero-sum example, but its image modulo2 is all of Z/2Z. These are two controls of **one CRT preservation route**, not two attempts. A valid fiber induction would need extra colored subset-sum information recording the number of generators, beyond ordinary cube freeness in each prime-power factor. The source's product/preimage examples do not supply that information.

## A3 — Reduce cube dimension through translated links

For x∈A define B_x=A∩(A−x). If B_x contained a (d−1)-cube, adjoining x would give a d-cube in A; thus the link is genuinely (d−1)-cube-free. This is a valid dimension-reduction lemma. Try to close Q1960 inductively using |B_x|≥2|A|−N.

Even granting the universal preceding-dimension bound |B_x|≤(d−2)N/(d−1), this yields only |A|≤[1−1/(2(d−1))]N. For d>2 this is weaker than [1−1/d]N. At d=4 the preceding 3-cube theorem actually is available for every cyclic order, and the result is5N/6, above the required3N/4. At other d the divisor requirement (d−1)|N need not even hold. Thus the correct link lemma plus the elementary intersection estimate does not furnish the claimed induction. A correlated estimate using several links, rather than two-set inclusion-exclusion, remains missing. This route fails by an exact coefficient loss, independently of CRT preservation and the diagonal obstruction.

## A4 — Obtain a full-density cube theorem by independent-form counting

Count d-cubes and try replacing the joint indicators of their 2^d−1 sums by independent vertex indicators, invoking Fourier uniformity from high density. At d=4,N=20q take A to be the nonzero residues modulo5. It has density4/5, above the target3/4. Four independent uniform generators are uniform modulo5. Exactly the four residue sequences (r,r,r,r), r=1,2,3,4, avoid a zero nonempty subset sum: direct enumeration of all5^4 possibilities verifies this and supplies an exact finite proof. Thus the actual cube probability is4/5^4, whereas the independent-form expression is(4/5)^15. The discrepancy is constant through every lift, and the Fourier coefficient at a character of order5 has normalized magnitude1/5.

This set does contain cubes, so it is not a counterexample. It shows that the proposed density-to-uniformity and moment-factorization steps are false even **above** the conjectured threshold. A valid counting proof would need to resolve structured low-order-character cases instead of discarding them as small Fourier error. The full link identities in A3 and the fractional/CRT questions are not substituted for this missing correlation analysis.

## A5 — Classify extremizers as complements of quotient fibers

The source's tight construction is the complement of the kernel of Z/NZ→Z/dZ. Try to prove that every maximum-cardinality cube-free set has this same quotient-fiber form; this would immediately determine its size and solve the bound. There are already noncoset extremizers. For d=4,N=12 let B={0,2,10} and A=Z/12Z\B, of size9=3N/4. Exact enumeration of all C(15,4)=1365 multisets of four generators gives433 distinct subset-sum sets; each meets B. This is a finite exhaustive proof of 4-cube freeness, independently replayed by the checker. Every free set omits0, and for each of the11 possible second omitted points b some cube avoids {0,b}; hence no complement of size2 works. A is therefore genuinely maximum-cardinality at these parameters.

The unique subgroup of order3 is {0,4,8}. Its four cosets are {r,r+4,r+8}, r=0,1,2,3; none equals B and B contains no complete coset. Unit automorphisms preserve that subgroup, so the difference is not removed by an automorphism or a coset choice. A quotient-fiber-only extremal classification is false. A classification valid only strictly above the threshold is a different sufficient theorem, still unproved; this witness does not refute it or the bound. The failure is of the proposed **extremizer classification** route, distinct from the local fiber induction in A2.
