# B1 continuation: proof, scope and stopping review

This review accepts a solution only at its stated scope. A stopped target
remains mathematically unresolved. Numbered full-target routes count once
whether they yield a counterexample to a proposed lemma or end at an exact
unproved obligation. Shared gaps are disclosed; they are not independent
impossibility theorems. Source searches, failed commands, checker cases and
agent reconnections add no attempts.
