# Main-thread review of Q2778

The five constructions in Q2778-ATTEMPTS.md are accepted as distinct
full-question routes. The exact examples are sufficient to refute their
proposed completeness lemmas, rather than the decidability question itself.

A1's token/owner construction handles overlapping definable classes by giving
each occurrence separate auxiliary tokens, and relativizes all original
quantifiers and local counts to O. Injections, bijections and unmatched tokens
give the stated homogeneous comparisons. A center with k distinct guarded
unmatched neighbors gives a lower bound k on positive surplus without
requiring a unique center. All of these are permitted local guarded counts.
Finite satisfiability consequently reduces to ordinary GP². Fixed nonzero
affine equality is excluded from this restricted theorem.

A2 has valid aggregate stubs at (2,4), but at most two directed internal
edges exist on two loop-free P vertices. Its (4,8) witness is simple.
A3 fails earlier in linear accounting: every original source contributes
one to |B|−2|C|, while a separated period vertex contributes zero. Labeling
offsets does not itself establish a safe exact-count merge. A4 cannot divide
a nonempty matching into half-vertices; a primitive full count vector need
not have scale coordinate one. A5 correctly shows that a single truth
profile can have infinitely many indecomposable multivariate count vectors,
even though its one-dimensional size projection is an additive semigroup.

The overlaps are disclosed and do not inflate the count: A1 attempts literal
internalization, A4 scaling; A2 exact multigraph lifting, A3 offset/period
splitting; A5 finite component generation. Repeated source warnings and finite
checker cases are not counted as extra attempts. The full question stops
at five failures. Its homogeneous/one-sided positive-surplus reduction is
accepted as a partial result. This is internal review, with no claim of
novelty or external peer review.
