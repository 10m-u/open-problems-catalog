The argument passes at that scope. Ordinary unary quantification produces
closed formulas; recording every such closed subformula gives finitely many
profiles. Positive binary guards exclude cross-summand witnesses. Every
local count and its integer comparison therefore remains unchanged on a
summand. A closed existential is the disjunction of the two summand truth
values, and those values agree by the profile hypothesis. Negation and
nested quantification are covered by structural induction. Models with the
same profile may consequently be added without changing the profile.

For a nonempty additive size set, choose its minimum m and the minimum
in every attained residue modulo m. Closure under adding m supplies every
larger value in that residue, exactly. This proves a finite union of
arithmetic progressions. A finite union over profiles proves Q2777.

The paper's multivariate quadratic spectrum does not invalidate this:
the corresponding vector monoid lies in N², where finite generation need
not hold. Its total-size projection and every other one-dimensional
profile monoid obey the elementary numerical argument. This neither
computes the residue minima nor solves Q2778.
