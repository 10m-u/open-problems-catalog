# Q1249 substantive attempts

## Q1249-H01: actual all-tail inputs against general Edrei factors

Attempt: disprove sufficiency with finite A of degree five through seven
whose tails are real-rooted, using TP inputs that have independently chosen
positive poles and negative-zero numerators. The bounded screen samples
1800 candidate A outside the q>=4 sufficient class, accepts 387 numerically,
and checks 30,960 actual Edrei input products. It finds no candidate passing
the exact counterexample gate. Floating acceptance is not a proof of the
tail hypothesis; any promoted counterexample would require exact root counts.
No counterexample or all-degree proof is obtained. First full-target failure.

## Q1249-H02: upgrade the necessary quotient bound to four

Attempt: derive q_j>=4 from all-tail LP-I, then apply Hutchinson to the
Hadamard product with every log-concave input. RESULTS.md gives an exact
all-tail cubic with q_2=100/27<4. Thus the proposed bridge is false even
in a source-proved low-degree case. The source's q_j>3 does not suffice
for the attempted Hutchinson proof. Second full-target failure.

## Q1249-H03: extend entire-input preservation by density

Attempt: use Malo preservation for all entire TP inputs, prove the shifted
single-pole subclass separately, and extend to every meromorphic Edrei input
by locally uniform approximation from entire TP inputs. The single-pole
theorem is proved in RESULTS.md for arbitrary multiplicity and shift.
The proposed approximation is impossible even for 1/(1-z): every entire
TP input obeys b_2<=b_1^2/(2b_0), whereas the geometric input violates it.
Neither the proved subclasses nor the density step covers arbitrary mixed
Edrei factors. Third full-target failure, with the subclass proof retained.

## Q1249-H04: a positive cone generated by LP-I tails

Attempt: express outputs for pole/numerator inputs as nonnegative sums of
tails and infer LP-I from the hypothesis on each tail. The exact all-tail
quadratic A=1+z+z^2/10 has A+100R_2 A non-real-rooted. The tail cone
is not contained in LP-I. The coefficient restrictions on a valid Edrei
input must be used; the bad weights themselves are outside that class.
This invalidates the proposed cone step and is not a target counterexample.
Fourth full-target failure.

## Q1249-H05: finite-degree proof followed by Taylor truncation

Attempt: preserve the all-tail hypothesis in finite Taylor sections, prove
finite cases, and take a locally uniform limit. RESULTS.md gives the exact
partial-theta example q=18/5: every infinite tail is LP-I, but the quadratic
Taylor section is non-real-rooted. Thus the needed hypothesis is lost even
before a finite-case argument. The source already proves finite degrees
through four; neither the attempted approximation nor the missing finite
all-degree theorem is supplied here. Fifth full-target failure: stop Q1249
unresolved. H03 varies the input B; H05 varies the hypothesized multiplier A.
