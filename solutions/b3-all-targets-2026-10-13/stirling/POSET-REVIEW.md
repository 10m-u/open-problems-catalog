For width-two weak orders, consecutive layer labelling has no boundary descents and exactly one optional descent in each two-element layer. Thus (1+t)^k is the descent polynomial and the displayed P-partition expansion is correct, independent of the positions of singleton layers. Its common factor includes all shifts 0,...,N-k-1. With x=t+(b-1)/2, logarithmic differentiation of the generating function gives Q_(k+1)=2xQ_k+k(k+b)Q_(k-1). This proves all ordinal sums of singleton/two-element layers, not every width-two poset.
