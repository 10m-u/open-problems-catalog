# Q3902: all reflexive crosspolytopes have a binary-code gamma formula

Local proof, 2026-10-13, accepted by independent root review. This is a
restricted all-size theorem, not the full Q3902 statement or a novelty
claim. The exact catalog target `ede55ed8ecc1ee3fb171` is [Question 9.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.17253v1)
of Donzelmann--Holleben--Juhnke. Its lattice is the integral lattice in
the real span, not merely the lattice generated by the vertices. The
local primary source is `texts/papers/collections-0030cf22a56ed52c.txt.gz`;
Sections 2, 5, 6 and 9 were read. Prior graph and finite computations
are not new solve attempts.

## Theorem and proof

Let P=conv(±a_1,...,±a_m), where the a_i are linearly independent integral
vectors, and use L=span_R(P) intersect Z^N. If P is reflexive in L,
there is an even binary linear code C in F_2^m such that

    h*_P(t) = sum_(c in C) 2^wt(c) t^(wt(c)/2)(1+t)^(m-wt(c)).       (1)

Consequently, in dimension m,

    gamma_j(P) = 4^j |{c in C : wt(c)=2j}| >= 0.                    (2)

Pull L back under the linear isomorphism A:e_i -> a_i. The resulting
lattice Lambda contains Z^m and lies in Q^m. In these coordinates P is
the ordinary unit l1 ball. Its facets have equations epsilon dot x=1,
for every epsilon in {±1}^m. Reflexivity is exactly the assertion that
all these sign vectors belong to Lambda's dual lattice.

For x in Lambda, subtract two such integer-valued sign functionals that
differ only in coordinate i. This proves 2x_i is integral. The all-positive
functional proves sum_i x_i is integral. Thus Lambda/Z^m is a binary
linear subgroup C, and every codeword has even weight. Conversely every
even binary subgroup gives integer values for all sign functionals, so
this describes precisely the reflexive lattices of the unit crosspolytope:

    Lambda = union_(c in C) (Z^m+c/2).

For c of weight w, the generating series by exact l1 norm of its coset
is a product of m one-coordinate series. An integer coordinate contributes
`(1+t)/(1-t)`, and a half-integer coordinate contributes
`2 t^(1/2)/(1-t)`. Since w is even, all norms are integers. Its series is
therefore

    2^w t^(w/2)(1+t)^(m-w)/(1-t)^m.

Summing over the distinct cosets counts every lattice point once.
Passing from exact norms to counts in kP divides by 1-t. Multiplication
by (1-t)^(m+1) gives (1). The terms already have the required gamma basis,
so (2) follows. This also proves that the vertex-lattice index is |C|,
and the normalized volume is `h*(1)=2^m |C|`.

For a symmetric **homology** polytope, vanishing top homology means
the top boundary columns are linearly independent. Thus every reflexive
homology polytope with H_d(Delta;Z)=0 satisfies the full requested gamma
inequality. This includes torsion cases; no unimodularity assumption
was used. For any independent-column cohomology configuration the same
matrix theorem applies, but it does not assert that general top
coboundary matrices have independent columns.

## Actual torsion calibration

Use the ten triangles
`125,126,134,136,145,234,235,246,356,456` of the source RP2 complex.
The integral top boundary has rank ten, rank nine modulo two, and a
maximal minor of absolute value two. Its maximal-minor gcd is therefore
two: all minors are even by the mod-two rank, and the displayed minor
precludes a larger gcd. Its pullback lattice has exactly two cosets.
The nontrivial codeword has weight ten; hence

    h*(t)=(1+t)^10+1024 t^5,
    gamma=(1,0,0,0,0,1024).

The checker records the minor and its row indices. This is an actual
boundary matrix example, not an arbitrary code realization claim. The
underlying reflexivity and torsion example are prior literature.

## Failed extension by nonnegative forest contributions

The attempted full proof was to express h*_P as a nonnegative scalar
combination of the h* polynomials of its full-rank spanning-forest
crosspolytopes. Formula (1) would then prove gamma positivity without
new signs. This extension is false even for the actual triangle graph.
Its three spanning trees have unimodular boundary bases and each has
`h*=(1+t)^2`. Its full symmetric edge polytope has integral coordinates
`conv(±e1,±e2,±(e1+e2))`. The inequalities are
`|x|<=1, |y|<=1, |x-y|<=1`. Literal counts in kP are
`3k(k+1)+1`, yielding `h*=1+4t+t^2` and gamma=(1,2).

Any nonnegative scalar combination of forest numerators has gamma_1=0,
so cannot equal that numerator. Even arbitrary signed scalar forest
weights cannot repair the missing basis term. A full proof needs new
intersection/half-open-cell contributions, rather than weighting whole
forest crosspolytopes alone. This is a failure of that developed
forest assembly contract, not a negative gamma coefficient or a failure
of the restricted theorem. It counts as one full-target attempt.
